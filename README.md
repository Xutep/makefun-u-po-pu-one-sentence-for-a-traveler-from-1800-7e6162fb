`main.py` (stdlib-only, deterministic) writes exactly one sentence of at most
200 characters to `/output/entry.txt`, explaining the modern internet to a
time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800 — using only words
and images a person of that year could already know: roads, letters, books,
songs, doors, horses.

The sentence:

> Picture every road on earth joined into one great road, so any letter,
> book, or song may reach any door in the blink of an eye, at any hour, with
> no horse at all.

Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
